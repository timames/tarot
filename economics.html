What it costs to run

Today: essentially nothing. Play Console is $25 once. The domain is about $12/year. Cloudflare Pages, AdMob and RevenueCat are all $0 at your scale — RevenueCat only starts charging (1%) above $2,500 monthly tracked revenue.

That puts break-even at roughly 3 daily users. Everything past that is margin.

If you ever add AI-written readings, the sums still work — but only if you pre-generate. Twelve signs × three periods, generated once daily on a cheap model and served to everyone, costs about $0.28/month total regardless of how many users you have.

Generating live, per user, per reading is where it gets tight: fine on a budget model (~$0.0003, covered 36× by one rewarded view), loss-making on a mid-tier model for free readings (~$0.006 against $0.0027 earned).
