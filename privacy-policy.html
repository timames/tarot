Mystic Oracle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Mystic Oracle ("the app") is a divination and entertainment app offering tarot readings, horoscopes, natal charts, numerology, and related features.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, and makes no network requests of its own.

Data stored on your device

If you enter a name, birth date, birth time, or birth location to use features such as the natal chart or numerology, that information is saved only on your device (in the app's local storage) so the forms can be prefilled next time. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Disclaimer

All readings, charts, and forecasts in the app are provided for entertainment and personal reflection only. They are not medical, legal, financial, or professional advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if advertising is added in a future version), this page will be updated before that version is released.
