Mystic Oracle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Mystic Oracle ("the app") is a divination and entertainment app offering tarot readings, horoscopes, natal charts, numerology, and related features. The app is free to use, is supported by advertising, and offers an optional paid subscription ("Mystic Oracle Plus").

Data stored on your device

If you enter a name, birth date, birth time, or birth location to use features such as the natal chart or numerology, that information is saved only on your device (in the app's local storage) so the forms can be prefilled next time. Your name, birth date, birth time and birth location are never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Freshly written readings

The app can write horoscopes and reading interpretations fresh instead of drawing them from a fixed set of pre-written text. This is done by an AI model running on Cloudflare's network, through a service we operate.

What is sent when this feature is on:

  • For horoscopes: nothing about you at all. Horoscopes are written once per day for all twelve signs and the same text is delivered to everyone, so your device simply downloads a file.
  • For a tarot reading: the names and positions of the cards that were drawn, and the name of the spread.
  • For a natal chart reading: the calculated astrological positions and aspects from your chart — not your name, birth date, birth time, or birth location.

What is never sent: your name, your date or place of birth, any text you type into the app, any account or advertising identifier, and any way of linking one reading to another. Requests carry no user identifier of any kind. As with any internet request, the receiving server sees the IP address it came from; it is used only to serve the request and to prevent abuse, and it is not stored alongside reading content.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Readings. With it off, the app produces every reading on your device from its built-in text and makes no network requests for readings at all. The app remains fully usable offline either way.

Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process device and usage information, including a device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad-interaction data. This is used to show ads, limit repetition, and measure performance. You can control ad personalization in your device settings, and subscribers to Mystic Oracle Plus see no ads. See Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases & subscriptions

Mystic Oracle Plus is sold through Google Play Billing and managed with the help of RevenueCat, which validates purchases and tells the app whether your subscription is active. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees your card or full billing details. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user ID and purchase/receipt information to manage entitlements. See revenuecat.com/privacy and Google Play's terms for details.

Data we do not collect

Aside from the advertising and purchase processing described above (handled by Google and RevenueCat), the app has no accounts of its own, runs no analytics of its own, and does not sell your personal data.

Deleting your data

Because the app keeps your information only on your device and holds no accounts, you can delete everything yourself, immediately, without contacting anyone:

  • In the app: open Settings → Reset all data. This permanently erases everything the app has stored — your profile, saved readings and spreads, streaks, collections, and preferences.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the same data.

There is no server-side copy of this data to delete: readings requests carry no identifier, and we keep no databases of users. For data processed by our service providers: the advertising identifier can be reset or deleted in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads), and subscription records held by Google Play and RevenueCat are governed by their policies linked above. If you have any data deletion question or request, contact tim.ames@gmail.com and we will assist, including relaying deletion requests to RevenueCat for subscribers.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Disclaimer

All readings, charts, and forecasts in the app are provided for entertainment and personal reflection only. They are not medical, legal, financial, or professional advice.
